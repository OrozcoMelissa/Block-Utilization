# Block-Utilization
Block Utilization
Block Utilization measures how much of the allocated OR time for a specialty or surgeon is actually used for surgical cases. Specialty acuity significantly affects block time metrics. High-acuity specialties require more complex scheduling, and misalignment can lead to inefficient block use and increased patient risk. The department's goal for block utilization is above 60% those blocks (surgeons or groups) not meeting the threshold are subject to losing allocated block times to schedule their procedures.
